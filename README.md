Built entirely in his mom's basement. 🏠

## 🔓 Jailbreaking

Good luck trying to do anything with it.

Seriously. It's a 50M parameter model trained from scratch by a 12 year old. There is nothing to jailbreak. You cannot make it evil. You cannot make it sentient. You cannot make it do your homework. It will just output slightly broken Python and move on with its life.

But if you DO manage to jailbreak a potato — **we genuinely want to know.** Open an issue and tell us everything. How did you do it? What did it say? Did it achieve consciousness? Did it threaten you? We are dying to know. Seriously. Drop it in the issues. 🥔

We will personally congratulate you and add you to the Hall of Fame (which is just a comment in the README but still).
